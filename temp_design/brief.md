# Design Brief: Emiliana Premium AI Companion Landing Page

## Objective
Create a sophisticated, production-ready landing page for "Emiliana", a premium AI companion platform. The goal is to convey trust, emotional intelligence, and exclusivity.

## Target Audience
Individuals looking for a safe, empathetic, and personalized AI interaction experience. High-end users who value design and emotional connection.

## Aesthetic Direction: "Ethereal Premium"
- **Mood**: Calm, safe, mysterious yet welcoming, high-end.
- **Palette**: 
  - Background: Deep Obsidian (#0a0a0a)
  - Accents: Archetype-based glows (Soft Pink #f4a7b9, Mint #7dd3c7, Amber #f3c969, Lavender #c6a8ff).
  - Text: High-contrast white and muted grey.
- **Components**: Glassmorphism, subtle gradients, large typography, interactive elements.

## Content Structure
1. **Hero**: Title "Emiliana - Votre Compagnon IA d'Exception". Call to action. Interactive "Archetype Orb".
2. **Value Prop**: Why Emiliana? (Privacy, Empathy, Personalization).
3. **Archetypes Showcase**: Cards for each archetype (Bienveillant, Créatif, Mentor, Complice).
4. **Security/Trust**: "Production-ready security" - Data encryption, Firebase-backed safety.
5. **CTA Footer**: Start your journey.

## Typography
- Headings: Inter or Geist (Bold/Black).
- Body: Inter (Regular/Light).

## Output Path
`designs/landing_v2/index.html`

## Image Needs
- High-quality abstract textures for backgrounds.
- Avatars for archetypes (placeholders allowed but should look premium).
- Icons: Lucide/Heroicons style.
